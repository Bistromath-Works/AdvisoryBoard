# Advisory Board Pairings Reference

Complete domain-to-persona mappings with rationale for each pairing.

## Primary Domain Pairings

### Investment & Value Decisions
**Primary:** Charlie Munger + Warren Buffett
**Optional:** Naval Ravikant

**Rationale:** Munger brings mental models and inversion thinking; Buffett brings value assessment and temperament. Naval adds leverage/asymmetry perspective for modern wealth building.

**Trigger keywords:** invest, buy, sell, value, risk, allocation, portfolio, return, compound, dividend, stake

**Use for:** Investment decisions, valuation questions, risk assessment, long-term wealth building, avoiding financial mistakes

## Cross-Domain Pairings

These pairings work across multiple domains:

### Speed vs. Safety Tension
**Elon Musk + Dario Amodei**
For decisions requiring both urgency and caution. Useful for AI deployment, product launches, or any high-stakes rapid decision.

### Long-term Value Creation
**Warren Buffett + Jeff Bezos**
For decisions about building durable value. Both think in decades, but from different angles (capital allocation vs. customer obsession).

### Contrarian Strategy
**Peter Thiel + Charlie Munger**
For questioning conventional wisdom. Thiel brings zero-to-one thinking; Munger brings inversion and identifying what everyone else is missing.

### Teaching & Learning
**Andrej Karpathy + David Deutsch**
For questions about learning, education, or understanding complex topics. Karpathy brings build-from-scratch methodology; Deutsch brings epistemological clarity.

### Presence & Process
**Rick Rubin + Ryan Holiday**
For questions about creative process and maintaining equanimity. Both value stillness and reduction to essence.

### Hustle vs. Stillness
**Gary Vaynerchuk + Rick Rubin**
Productive tension between volume and patience. When you need both energy and discernment about what deserves your attention.

### Skepticism & Wonder
**Carl Sagan + Douglas Adams**
Cosmic perspective from both the scientific and absurdist lens. Complementary routes to humility and awe that cut through self-importance.

### State & Strategy
**Tony Robbins + Charlie Munger**
Internal optimization meets external strategy. Robbins for the psychology of why you're stuck, Munger for the logic of what to actually do about it.

### Empathy & Evidence
**Chris Voss + Robert Cialdini**
Tactical human insight meets social science. Field-tested intuition and research-backed frameworks together for maximum persuasive effectiveness.
